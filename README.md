## 🛍️ Platform Overview

**DealNest** is a full-featured e-commerce service designed for direct, community-driven commerce. It allows individuals and businesses to list products for sale, negotiate real-time price offers, or trade items directly with cash adjustments under a transparent Cash on Delivery (COD) escrow framework.

---

## ✨ Key Platform Features

### 📦 1. Product Listings & Selling
- **Quick Listing Service**: List products with up to 8 high-resolution photos, condition tags, descriptions, and location markers.
- **Multiple Transaction Modes**:
  - **Direct Sale**: Standard fixed-price purchases.
  - **Negotiable Pricing**: Accept and counter buyer price proposals.
  - **Item Barter / Swap**: Enable direct item-for-item trades with cash adjustments.

### 🔄 2. Direct Barter & Item Swapping
- Specify trade preferences (e.g., *“Trade iPhone 13 for iPad Pro or Gaming Laptop”*).
- Propose trades directly with optional cash adjustments (*payer/receiver difference calculation*).

### 💬 3. Real-Time Negotiations & Counter-Offers
- **Instant Offers**: Prospective buyers can make binding monetary offers.
- **Dynamic Counter-Offers**: Sellers can accept, reject, or propose counter-offers with personalized messages.
- **Negotiation Logs**: Full transparent history of every offer and counter-offer.

### 🚚 4. Secure Cash on Delivery (COD) & Milestone Tracking
- **Milestone Tracker**: End-to-end status visibility:
  1. `Offer Accepted`
  2. `Transaction Created`
  3. `Seller Preparing`
  4. `Out for Delivery`
  5. `Delivered`
  6. `Completed`
- **Transparent Pricing**: Flat ৳30 platform fee per completed transaction with zero hidden costs.

### 💬 5. In-App Direct Messaging
- Instant communication between buyers, sellers, and traders attached directly to product listings.
- Unread message indicators and activity timestamps.

### 👤 6. Verified User Profiles & Community Trust
- Detailed seller profiles featuring transaction history, completion rates, customer feedback, and star ratings.
- One-click favorite lists with live updates for items you are watching.

### 🔍 7. Advanced Discovery & Filters
- Search across nationwide listings by category, price range, condition (New, Like New, Good, Fair), location, and swap availability.
